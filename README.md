## 🧭 How I Approach Building

1. **Understand the problem.** Identify who needs the solution and what they need to accomplish.
2. **Make the idea concrete.** Sketch the interface, workflow, and information involved.
3. **Build a useful first version.** Connect the essential parts.
4. **Test what actually happens.** Look at behaviour, errors, and user feedback.
5. **Improve with evidence.** Refine the experience and strengthen the system.

I value curiosity, clear communication, practical progress, and the patience to keep improving.
